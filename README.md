# Travis Seah · personal website

Live at **https://ridenowtravis.github.io/travis-seah-website/**

## Changing the words
1. On GitHub, open `content.json` and click the pencil icon.
2. Edit the text inside the quotes. Keep the quotes and commas.
3. Click **Commit changes**. The site rebuilds itself in about a minute (see the **Actions** tab).

Anything in `[square brackets]` is treated as a placeholder and blocks publishing until it's filled in.

## Adding a photo
Upload it into an `assets/` folder (e.g. `assets/travis.jpg`), then set `"photo": "assets/travis.jpg"` in `content.json`.

## Preview on your computer (optional)
`node server.js`, then open http://localhost:4000

## Compliance
Get PropNex compliance approval before sharing the link in ads or flyers. The footer already shows the CEA registration number, agency name and licence number.
